Red Comet
2024
Fanart for "Mobile Suit Gundam: 0079" depicting my favourite character from the show. I wanted to capture his impishness, which I think is his most appealing trait. It's often downplayed in more modern "Gundam" media. Once I was tabling at an event and an older japanese lady pointed at a print of this one and was like "I remember seeing him on the TV when I was little". Talking to her was heartwarming... Is this the benefit of liking old shows?!
