Malevolant Antipode
2024
Commission turnaround: 2 Weeks.
Illustration for a CCG about mechs and their pilots. Don't think it's out yet. Designed a pilot and a mech.
The concept was that he's a former ace for an imperial power that was abandoned in a giant garbage dump. Then he became some kind of mad-max esque figure... The mech is a monster of garbage and flesh.
I had a lot of fun drawing his big scary robot arm.
