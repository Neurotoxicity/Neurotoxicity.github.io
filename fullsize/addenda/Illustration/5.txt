Metal Word Key Art
2024
Turnaround: 1 week.
Commission - key art for an upcoming "Heavy Metal Gaming Anthology" called "The Metal Word". Designed the main character, a helicopter for him to fight, and a logo.
Brief was for a muscular but unfinished looking cyborg man (a la "The Maxx"). I got really hung up on having his midsection be nothing but a spine because I thought it'd contrast well with muscular limbs...
