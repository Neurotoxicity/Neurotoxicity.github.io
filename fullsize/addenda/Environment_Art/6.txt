Cathedral
2022
Illustration for a story I was working on about King Ghidorah. The idea was the kaiju arrives on earth, is the size of the moon, and basically lays waste to the joint. And then after he leaves, weird fungi mutated by his influence appear and take over... Earth is terraformed, slowly, into a womb from which another King Ghidorah will be born...
It was based on a nightmare I had during COVID.
